Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

IntentFlow ("the App") is a Shopify application operated by the IntentFlow team ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect through the Shopify API when you install or use the App, how we use it, whether we share it with third parties, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect via the Shopify API

When you install the App, we request only the minimum access scopes required:

  • Shop information — your shop domain (*.myshopify.com) and an API access token, used to authenticate API calls and associate your account.
  • Product data (read_products) — product titles, handles, prices, images and URLs, used to generate product recommendations and to detect broken or out-of-stock landing pages linked from your ads.

We do not request access to your customers' personal data, and we do not collect, store, or process end-customer personal information through the App.

2. How We Use the Information

  • To provide the App's core functionality: ad stop-loss monitoring, broken-link interception, and product recommendations.
  • To authenticate your shop and maintain your subscription status.
  • To diagnose errors and improve the App.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. How We Store and Protect Data

  • Shopify API access tokens are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and are never written to disk or logs in plain text.
  • Data is transmitted over HTTPS only.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to operate the App:

  • Vercel Inc. — hosting of the App's web servers.
  • IntentFlow backend API (intentflowapp.com) — processes shop and product data to generate recommendations.

We do not share your data with any other third parties except where required by law (e.g., a valid court order).

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • When you uninstall the App, the app/uninstalled webhook triggers deletion of your sessions and shop data from our systems.
  • We honor Shopify's mandatory GDPR webhooks: customers/data_request, customers/redact, and shop/redact. Shop-related data is deleted within 30 days of a redaction request.

6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing us. Because we do not store end-customer personal data, requests relating to your customers are confirmed and processed through Shopify's GDPR webhook flow described above.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@intentflowapp.com
