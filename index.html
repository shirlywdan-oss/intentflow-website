WHY IT MATTERS

A campaign can keep running after its destination stops selling.

01

URLs change

Deleted products and changed handles can leave ads pointing at missing pages.

02

Inventory changes

Products and specific variants can become unavailable while campaigns remain active.

03

Redirects drift

A working redirect can still send paid traffic to the wrong destination.

HOW IT WORKS

A clear path from detection to verification.

1

Add landing pages

Add one URL, paste a list, or import a CSV. Platform, campaign, ad name, and ad ID are optional organizational fields.

2

Run an on-demand scan

Check registered URLs for missing pages, sold-out products, unexpected redirects, unavailable variants, and responses that cannot be confirmed.

3

Estimate the exposure

Enter CPC and estimated daily clicks. IntentFlow calculates the estimate transparently without guessing or importing ad-platform data.

4

Record and verify the fix

Record the action taken, scan again, and retain before-and-after evidence when the page is healthy.

CORE FEATURES

Focused tools for landing-page risk.

Flexible URL intake

Register landing pages individually, by bulk paste, or through CSV import.

Transparent classification

See issue type, confidence, first detection, duration, and recent scan evidence.

Merchant-controlled estimates

Use your own CPC and click estimates to calculate spend at risk.

Action history

Record whether you paused an ad, replaced a URL, added a redirect, restocked, or changed a variant.

Verification workflow

Re-scan after action and distinguish verified fixes from issues that still fail.

Platform labels

Organize URLs with Meta, TikTok, Google, or Other labels without connecting an ad account.

BUILT FOR CLARITY

What IntentFlow Ads does—and does not do.

  • Does: scan the landing-page URLs that you register.
  • Does: calculate estimates from values you enter.
  • Does not: connect to or automatically discover ads.
  • Does not: access actual spend, clicks, conversions, or ROAS.
  • Does not: automatically pause campaigns or change ad destinations.
